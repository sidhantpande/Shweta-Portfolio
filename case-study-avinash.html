Section 1 — Who is Coach Avinash?

Avinash Anand Singh is one of India's most prominent Transformational Coaches, Keynote Speakers, and educators — specialising in the Law of Attraction and the subconscious mind. He is the Founder and Lead Coach of Blue Dot Transform Consulting Pvt. Ltd. (BDTC), based in Pune, Maharashtra.

His work sits at the intersection of science and spirituality — blending neuroplasticity, quantum physics, and positive psychology with ancient spiritual practices. He is a former Forbes Coaches Council member (2020), has contributed to Forbes and Business World magazine, spoken at the United Nations MUN conference and the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII), and was awarded the REX Karmaveer Global Fellowship in partnership with the United Nations.

He is a Professional Certified Coach (ICF), Certified Neuroplasticity Coach, and an Integrated Clinical Hypnotherapist. His coaching and workshops have impacted 15,000+ people across 26 cities and 5+ countries. Managing a brand of this stature requires more than just scheduling posts; it requires deep philosophical alignment and intellectual rigour.

Section 2 — The Mandate

Shweta joined Blue Dot Transform Consulting as Associate Manager in March 2026 — responsible for the complete social media, content, and brand communications function for Coach Avinash across Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

This is not a narrow social media management role. It encompasses the full content lifecycle: strategy, scripting, graphic design, video direction, social media management, and performance reporting — all in service of building one of India's most credible personal brands in the transformation and coaching space. The goal is to translate complex metaphysical and scientific concepts into digestible, high-impact content that resonates with a global audience.

Section 5 — The Context: Why This Work Matters

Managing a personal brand for a coach of this profile is fundamentally different from managing an institutional brand. Every piece of content carries the weight of the individual's credibility, philosophy, and reputation. Scripts need to reflect his voice precisely, capturing the nuance of his expertise while remaining accessible to a broad audience.

Visuals need to match the gravitas of someone who has spoken at the UN, while social media engagement needs to feel personal — because for a transformational coach, community trust is the product. Shweta manages this entire function as a single integrated system — not as isolated deliverables, but as a coherent brand communication engine that powers one of India's most influential coaching mandates.
